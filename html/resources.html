Real people, real hotlines, real help

Crisis & domestic violence

National Domestic Violence Hotline

24/7 confidential support, safety planning, and referrals by phone, chat, or text.

Call 1-800-799-7233 · Text START to 88788

thehotline.org

Love Is Respect

Support for teens and young adults (14-24) navigating dating abuse and unhealthy relationships.

Call 1-866-331-9474 · Text LOVEIS to 22522

loveisrespect.org

StrongHearts Native Helpline

Culturally-specific, confidential support for Native American and Alaska Native survivors of domestic or sexual violence.

Call or text 1-844-762-8483

strongheartshelpline.org

RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline

24/7 confidential support for survivors of sexual assault, in English and Spanish.

Call 1-800-656-4673 · Text HOPE to 64673

rainn.org/hotline
