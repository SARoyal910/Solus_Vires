About

Who runs this site

Solus Vires is built and run by Steven Royal, through Omnia Royal LLC. It's an independent project, not a government service and not a domestic violence agency. The intent is to find a nonprofit or advocacy partner to review it and, eventually, help run it.

Why it exists

Help that's easy to find and safe to use

People being hurt by someone close to them often have little time, a shared or watched phone, and no idea where to start. This site tries to put trustworthy information and real hotlines in one discreet place, for anyone, whatever their gender, and to offer a private, encrypted notebook that even the people running the site can't read.

What it is, and isn't

Please read this

It is

  • Plain-language information, with every hotline and organization checked against its own website
  • Private, encrypted notes that only you can unlock
  • Optional check-ins that alert someone you choose if you miss one
  • Free, with no ads, no trackers, and no account needed to read anything

It isn't

  • Not an emergency service. Nobody here can send help. In danger, call 911.
  • Not legal advice, and not a substitute for an advocate or lawyer.
  • Not yet reviewed by legal counsel or a domestic violence advocacy organization. We are seeking both reviews and will say here when they are done. Until then, accounts are invite-only.
  • Not a registry. It will never publish or list people accused of abuse.

Your privacy

What we keep, and what we can't see

The short version: we can't read your notes, we don't use trackers or ads, and we don't keep logs of who visits which page. The full details are on the privacy page.

Getting support

If you need to talk to someone

This site doesn't offer direct support or counseling. Trained advocates at the National Domestic Violence Hotline do, free and 24/7: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.

If you are in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number).
