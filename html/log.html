Entries

Change your PIN

Every note, photo and your safety plan is re-encrypted on this device under the new PIN and saved in one step. If anything goes wrong partway, nothing changes and your current PIN keeps working. Larger vaults take a minute; keep this page open until it says it's done.

Write the new PIN down in the same safe place as your recovery codes. Nobody can reset it, including us.

Print or save a copy

This copy is not encrypted. A printout, a saved PDF, or a downloaded file can be read by anyone who finds it, and may sync to cloud storage. Keep it somewhere safe, or give it straight to your advocate or lawyer.

To make a PDF, choose Print, then Save as PDF where your device offers it. On iPhone, pinch outward on the print preview, then use Share to save it. The text file leaves out photos.

Quick Exit is available, but it cannot erase browser, network, or device history. This page locks itself after a few minutes of inactivity.
