Safety planning

A safety plan is personal. These prompts are starting points, not instructions that fit every situation.

Before a crisis

  • Identify safe contacts and code words.
  • Keep copies of key documents where they cannot be easily found.
  • Plan transportation and a safe destination if leaving becomes necessary.

Digital safety

  • Consider whether the device, browser, cloud account, or phone plan is monitored.
  • Use a safe device for research when possible.
  • Review location sharing settings before using any tracking feature.

Documentation

  • Store evidence somewhere the abusive person cannot access.
  • Preserve dates, screenshots, photos, messages, and incident notes if safe.
  • Ask an advocate or attorney about local rules before sharing evidence.

Family and children

  • Practice simple safety steps with children without increasing fear.
  • Share pickup restrictions with schools or childcare when appropriate.
  • Keep emergency contacts current.
Quick Exit is available, but it cannot erase browser, network, or device history.
