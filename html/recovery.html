Recovery & wellness

A framework, not a schedule

Three stages survivors commonly move through

Based on psychologist Judith Herman's widely used trauma-recovery model. People move back and forth between these — that's normal, not a setback.

1. Safety & stabilization

Establishing physical and emotional safety comes first — everything else builds on it.

  • Build or revisit your safety plan
  • Identify safe people, places, and routines
  • Stabilize sleep, food, and daily structure where possible

2. Remembrance & mourning

Processing what happened, at a pace you control — often with professional support.

  • Consider talking to a trauma-informed therapist (see directory below)
  • Use the private Notes tool if writing helps
  • Grief, anger, and numbness are all normal parts of this stage

3. Reconnection & growth

Rebuilding a sense of self and relationships on your own terms.

  • Reconnect with interests, people, or routines at your own pace
  • Peer support groups can help (see directory below)
  • Growth doesn't mean forgetting or "getting over it"

Real help, verified

Free and low-cost mental health resources

National, currently-operating organizations. See Resources for crisis, legal, and population-specific contacts.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential 24/7 support for suicidal, mental health, or emotional distress.

Call or text 988

Crisis Text Line

Free, 24/7 text-based crisis support with a trained counselor.

Text HOME to 741741

SAMHSA National Helpline

Free, confidential 24/7 treatment referral and information service for mental health and substance use.

Call 1-800-662-4357

samhsa.gov

SAMHSA FindTreatment.gov

Federal locator for mental health and substance-use treatment near you — filter by free or sliding-scale providers.

findtreatment.gov

NAMI HelpLine

Free, peer-staffed support for mental health questions. Not a crisis line — weekdays 10am-10pm ET only. Use 988 outside those hours.

Call 1-800-950-6264

nami.org

Open Path Collective

Directory of vetted therapists offering steeply reduced-rate sessions. Not free: a one-time $65 membership, then roughly $30-$80 per session.

openpathcollective.org

211

Connects you to local community services, including mental health support, in your area.

Call or text your ZIP code to 898211

211.org
