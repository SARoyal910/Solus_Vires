Emergency options

Use the safest option available in the moment. This site does not currently dispatch emergency responders.

Real-time location sharing and emergency response features must be opt-in, transparent, tested, and connected to trusted response partners before launch.

Call emergency services

If you are in immediate danger and can safely speak, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Call 911

Text when available

Some areas support text-to-911. If it is unavailable, you may receive a bounce-back message.

Text 911

Share with a trusted contact

A future version can support temporary location sharing, but it must never be hidden or enabled without consent.

Emergency features should be tested with advocates, public-safety experts, and legal counsel before release.
