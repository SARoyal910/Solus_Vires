About this site

Reaching the person who runs it

For questions about the site itself, not for support, see the About page. A monitored contact address is being set up and will be listed there.

For questions about the site itself, or to tell us something is wrong or missing. Messages go by email to the one person who runs this site (see the About page). This is not a support service: we aim to reply within 7 days if you leave a safe way to reach you, and it can take longer.

Email isn't private. Write only what you'd be OK with someone else reading, and only give a way to reach you that's safe. This site doesn't keep a copy; your message passes through our email provider (Brevo) to the inbox.

Not for emergencies. No one watches this inbox around the clock. If you need support now, call 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788 (National Domestic Violence Hotline, 24/7). In danger now? Call 911.
