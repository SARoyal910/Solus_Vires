Advocacy partner space

The partner system should be invite-based, verified, and audited before it handles referrals or survivor information.

Organization profiles

Verified services, languages, regions served, hours, eligibility, and safe contact methods.

Resource publishing

Partner-submitted resources should require moderation, review dates, and clear ownership.

Referral workflows

Future referrals need consent, encryption, role-based access, retention limits, and audit logs.

Partner access should never expose survivor data without explicit consent and a clear safety purpose.
